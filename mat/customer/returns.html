Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Here is our policy regarding refunds and returns:

1. Fresh Produce & Perishables

Fresh items like vegetables, fruits, bakery goods, and dairy products can be returned directly at the time of delivery if the quality is not up to standard. The delivery driver will adjust the billing amount or record a refund request instantly.

2. Packaged Goods

For packaged household items, snacks, or cooking essentials, we offer a 7-day return policy. The packaging must remain sealed, unopened, and in its original state.

3. Refund Credit

Refunds will be processed back to the original payment source (UPI, Credit Card, or Wallet) within 3-5 working days. Cash on delivery refunds will be credited as Mathura Quick Mart Store Wallet credits.
