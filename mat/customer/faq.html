Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the delivery timings for Mathura Quick Mart? ▼
We deliver from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day, including Sundays and public holidays. Orders placed after 9:30 PM will be delivered the next morning.
2. Is there a delivery fee for orders? ▼
Delivery is absolutely FREE for all orders with no minimum order value!
3. How can I track my order live? ▼
Once you place an order, go to your Customer Dashboard, click on "My Orders", and select the "Track" button to view real-time delivery rider positioning on our interactive map.
4. What is the return/refund policy? ▼
We offer a hassle-free 7-day return policy for sealed packaged goods. Fresh fruits, vegetables, milk, and bakery items can be returned at the time of delivery if you are unsatisfied with the quality.
